◆ ABOUT THIS TOOL

Find and Replace, Without the Surprises

Plain-text mode replaces exactly what you type — no special characters to escape. Regex mode gives you the full power of JavaScript regular expressions.

In the default plain text mode, characters like . * ? ( ) $ are matched literally and the replacement is inserted exactly as typed. Match case and Whole words narrow the search; whole-word matching is Unicode-aware, so it works for accented and non-Latin words too.

Need line breaks? With \n \t escapes on, type \n in either field for a newline and \t for a tab — for example replace , with \n to turn a comma-separated list into one item per line. Type \\ for a literal backslash.

Tick Regular expression to search with a pattern. The replacement supports $1…$99 for capture groups, $<name> for named groups, $& for the whole match and $$ for a literal dollar sign.

Patterns run in a background thread with a time limit. A pattern with catastrophic backtracking — such as (a+)+$ — is stopped after a couple of seconds with a clear message instead of freezing your browser tab.

⌕ REGEX CHEAT SHEET

Useful Patterns

Tick “Regular expression”, then try one of these.

^

Prefix every line

Find ^, replace with - to turn lines into a bullet list. $ appends to every line instead.

()

Swap with groups

Find (\w+), (\w+), replace with $2 $1: “Lovelace, Ada” → “Ada Lovelace”.

\s

Collapse spaces

Find [ \t]{2,}, replace with a single space. Find \s+$ with nothing to strip trailing whitespace.

\d

Reformat dates

Find (\d{4})-(\d{2})-(\d{2}), replace with $3/$2/$1: 2026-10-07 → 07/10/2026.

In regex mode, parts of the pattern in parentheses are numbered from 1. Use $1, $2… in the replacement to insert them, $<name> for named groups like (?<year>\d{4}), and $& for the whole match. In plain mode, $ is just a dollar sign.
