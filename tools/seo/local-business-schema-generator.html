    ◆ ABOUT THIS TOOL

    What Is Local Business Structured Data?

    Tell search engines your business's name, address, hours and contact details in a format they can't misread.

    LocalBusiness structured data describes a physical business that customers visit or that serves a local area: its name, address, phone number, opening hours, location on the map and price range. Google can use it for business details in Search and Maps, alongside your Google Business Profile.

    Google requires the name and address. Geo coordinates, telephone, URL, opening hours and price range are recommended — and restaurants can add cuisine, menu and reservations.

    Pick the most specific type you can — Dentist, Restaurant, HairSalon — rather than the general LocalBusiness. Each location of a chain should have its own page and its own markup.

    Keep the details identical to your Google Business Profile and the text on your page. For the company as a whole (logo, social profiles, legal name), use Organization markup on the home page.

    ✓ RULES

    Google's Local Business Guidelines

    What to include and how to format it.

    !

    Required

    name and address (a PostalAddress). Fill in as many address parts as apply: street, city, region, postal code, country.

    📍

    geo

    Latitude and longitude with at least 5 decimal places — roughly 1 metre precision. Copy them from Google Maps (right-click the pin).

    🕘

    openingHoursSpecification

    Days with opening and closing times in 24-hour format. Open all day: 00:00–23:59. Closed days are simply left out.

    ☎

    Phone & price

    telephone with the country and area code. priceRange such as “$$” or a range — under 100 characters.
