    ◆ ABOUT THIS TOOL

    What Is Product Structured Data?

    Give search engines your product's price, stock and rating — the details shown in product snippets.

    Product structured data describes an item you sell or review: its name, images, brand and identifiers, the offer (price, currency, availability, condition) and any ratings and reviews. Google uses it for product snippets — star ratings, price and stock shown with your result — and to understand product pages.

    Google requires a product name plus at least one of offers, review or aggregateRating. Everything else is recommended.

    This generator builds a single-product page with one Offer. Product rich results remain fully supported in 2026; online stores can also qualify for merchant listing experiences, which need extra shipping and return details best managed through Google Merchant Center.

    Marking up a review article rather than a shop page? Use the pros & cons section — Google can show them in the snippet for editorial reviews. Add a breadcrumb for the category path.

    Google's Product Snippet Rules

    The parts that most often cause errors.

    !

    Required

    name, and at least one of offers, review or aggregateRating. An Offer needs a price (a number; 0 is allowed).

    €

    Price & currency

    Use a plain number for price and an ISO 4217 code for priceCurrency. A priceValidUntil date in the past can stop the snippet from showing.

    ★

    Ratings & reviews

    aggregateRating needs ratingValue and ratingCount or reviewCount. Reviews must be genuine and visible on the page.

    ±

    Pros & cons

    Only for editorial product reviews, with at least two statements in total. Not for merchant pages with customer reviews.
