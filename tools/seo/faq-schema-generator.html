    FAQPage Structured Data in 2026

    The markup is still valid and still read by search engines — but Google no longer turns it into a rich result.

    FAQPage structured data labels the questions and answers on a page so machines can understand them: each question is a Question with an acceptedAnswer. This generator writes that JSON-LD for you, escapes everything correctly and flags problems before you publish.

    Important: as of 7 May 2026, Google no longer shows FAQ rich results (the expandable questions under a search result) for any site. In 2023 they had already been limited to well-known government and health websites; Google has also announced that the FAQ report and Rich Results Test support are being removed.

    So is FAQ markup still worth adding? Google says it continues to use FAQ structured data to understand pages, keeping it causes no problems, and other search engines and AI assistants can read it too. It's worth having on pages that genuinely are question-and-answer content — help centres, product support, policy pages.

    What it won't do any more is add extra space to your Google listing. Don't add FAQ blocks to pages just for the markup; spend that effort on types that still produce rich results, such as Product, Article and Local Business.

    ✓ RULES

    FAQPage Content Rules

    From Google's FAQPage guidelines — they still describe what valid markup looks like.

    👁

    Visible on the page

    Every question and the full answer must be visible to users on the page itself. Don't mark up content that's hidden or doesn't exist.

    1:1

    One answer per question

    FAQPage is for questions with a single answer written by the site. User-submitted answers (forums, support communities) are QAPage instead.

    </>

    Allowed answer HTML

    <h1>–<h6>, <br>, <ol>, <ul>, <li>, <a>, <p>, <div>, <b>, <strong>, <i>, <em>. Other tags are flagged.

    🚫

    No advertising

    Questions and answers must not be promotional, and the same question shouldn't be marked up on many pages of your site.
