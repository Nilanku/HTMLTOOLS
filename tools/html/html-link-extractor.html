◆ ABOUT THIS TOOL

About HTML Link Extraction

Audit link profiles, extract references, and verify on-page navigational structures.

Webpages frequently contain hundreds of hyperlinks spread across navigations, footer sitemaps, articles, and call-to-action buttons. Manually finding every target URL, verifying relative paths, and checking anchor texts for SEO optimization is tedious.

The Toolyard HTML Link Extractor parses raw HTML strings, identifies all <a> and <area> tags, resolves relative URLs against your specified Base URL, detects internal vs. external destinations, and exports a structured spreadsheet without sending any data over a network.

⚙ AUDIT & RESOLUTION

Link Detection & Classification

How links, anchors, and targets are processed by the extraction engine.

🔗

Relative & Absolute URL Resolution

When an optional Base URL (e.g. https://example.com) is provided, relative paths like /about or ../docs are converted into fully qualified absolute URLs automatically.

🏷

Internal vs. External Classification

Compares destination domain hostnames to separate internal site paths from external third-party links, allowing targeted audits of inbound or outbound references.

⚡

Special Protocols & Hash Anchors

Identifies jump links (#section), JavaScript triggers (javascript:void(0)), and contact handlers (mailto:, tel:), tagging them for easy filtering.
