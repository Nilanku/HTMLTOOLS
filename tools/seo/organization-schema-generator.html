    ◆ ABOUT THIS TOOL

    What Is Organization Structured Data?

    Help Google show the right logo, name and details for your brand.

    Organization structured data describes the company, non-profit or institution behind a website: its name, logo, website, social profiles, contact details and official identifiers. Google can use it to choose the logo shown for your site in search results and in knowledge panels, and to tell your organization apart from others with similar names.

    Google has no required properties for Organization markup — add all the ones that apply. Put it on your home page or a single “about us” page; you don't need it on every page.

    The sameAs links are especially useful: listing your official LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Wikipedia and Wikidata pages helps search engines connect them to you.

    If your business has a shop, office or restaurant that customers visit, use Local Business markup for that location. For the site name shown in Google, add WebSite markup on your home page too.

    ✓ GUIDELINES

    Google's Organization Recommendations

    The properties that matter most.

    🖼

    logo

    At least 112 × 112 pixels, crawlable and indexable. It should look as intended on a plain white background. JPG, PNG, GIF, SVG and WebP work.

    🔗

    url & sameAs

    Your website's URL, plus links to official profiles on other sites — social networks, review sites, Wikipedia, Wikidata.

    ☎

    contactPoint

    Ways to reach a specific department: type, phone (with country code), email, area served and languages.

    #

    Identifiers

    vatID, taxID, duns, leiCode, globalLocationNumber, naics, iso6523Code — they help identify your organization exactly.
